## Editorial guidelines

- **Start from the decision.** Every post answers "what decision does this help me make?"
- **Plain English first,** jargon defined on use. No acronym without expansion.
- **Templates beat theory.** Include a table, checklist, or worked example where possible.
- **One idea per post.** Split rather than sprawl.
- **Always set `level`, `categories`, `tags`, `weight`** so the path and topics stay coherent.

## Suggested cadence

- **Publish:** 1 post / 1–2 weeks. Finish one level's posts before starting the next.
- **Maintain:** revisit published posts quarterly; refresh numbers and frameworks.
- **Promote:** each post links to the previous and next on the path, and to the topic page.

## SEO notes (light touch)

- Titles use the phrases people actually search: "risk register", "risk appetite",
  "ISO 27005", "KRIs", "three lines model".
- `description` front matter is the meta description — keep it under ~155 chars.
- Internal linking is the main SEO lever: path neighbours + topic pages.
- The `/resources/` glossary page is the natural hub for definition-style queries.
