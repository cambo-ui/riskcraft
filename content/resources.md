## Glossary — the words that trip people up

| Term | Plain-English meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Risk** | The effect of uncertainty on objectives. In IT: the chance a technology outcome hurts a business goal. |
| **Threat** | Something that could cause harm (a ransomware crew, a flood, a resignation). |
| **Vulnerability** | A weakness a threat can exploit (unpatched server, missing MFA, unclear process). |
| **Likelihood** | How often/probably an event occurs, given current conditions. |
| **Impact** | How bad it is when it does — financial, operational, legal, reputational. |
| **Inherent risk** | The risk *before* you apply any controls. |
| **Residual risk** | The risk *remaining after* controls. This is what you actually accept. |
| **Risk appetite** | How much risk you are willing to take to meet objectives. |
| **Risk tolerance** | The acceptable range around that appetite. |
| **KRI** | Key Risk Indicator — a metric that warns a risk is getting worse. |
| **KPI** | Key Performance Indicator — a metric that shows how well you are performing. |

## Frameworks cheat sheet

| Framework | What it's for | Best when |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **ISO 31000** | Universal risk management principles | You need a language and process, not controls |
| **ISO 27005** | Information security risk management | You're aligning infosec risk to ISO 27001 |
| **NIST RMF** | US federal risk lifecycle (SP 800-37) | You want a mature, repeatable lifecycle |
| **NIST CSF** | Cyber security posture (Identify→Recover) | You need a board-level maturity view |
| **COBIT** | IT governance and management | You're bridging IT and enterprise governance |
| **COSO ERM** | Enterprise risk, strategy-aligned | Board/executive risk programme |
| **FAIR** | Quantitative cyber/tech risk analysis | You must express risk in money |
