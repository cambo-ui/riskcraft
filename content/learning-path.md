The path below is the spine of the blog. Each level has a goal, concrete outcomes, and the posts that take you there. **Published** posts are linked; **planned** posts are the roadmap I am writing toward.

You do not have to start at Level 0 — but if any of the vocabulary feels fuzzy, do. Everything later assumes you can explain likelihood, impact, inherent risk, and residual risk without reaching for a glossary.
