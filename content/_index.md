**RiskCraft** is a personal blog about **IT risk management** — written for anyone who has to make, explain, or defend decisions about technology risk inside a real organisation.

Most risk material is either academic or a sales pitch. This is neither. Every post is practical, opinionated where it matters, and mapped to a learning path that takes you from *"what even is risk?"* to *"I run the risk programme"*.
