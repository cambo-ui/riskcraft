Hi, I'm the author of **RiskCraft**.

I work in IT risk and governance, and I started this blog for one reason: the gap between how risk is *described* in textbooks and how it actually gets *done* inside an organisation is enormous.

Most teams do not fail at risk because they lack a framework. They fail because the framework is not connected to a decision anyone cares about. This blog is my attempt to close that gap — practically, honestly, and without vendor spin.

## What you'll find here

- A structured **zero-to-hero learning path** through IT risk.
- **Frameworks** (ISO 27005, NIST RMF, COBIT, COSO, FAIR) explained in plain language with worked examples.
- **Operating models** for governance, appetite, KRIs, and reporting.
- **Career notes** on certifications and moving from analyst to leader.
