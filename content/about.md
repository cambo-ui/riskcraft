**RiskCraft** is a blog about **IT risk management** — written by the **RiskCraft Team** for anyone who has to make, explain, or defend decisions about technology risk inside a real organisation.

We work in IT risk and governance, and we started this blog for one reason: the gap between how risk is *described* in textbooks and how it actually gets *done* inside an organisation is enormous.

Most teams do not fail at risk because they lack a framework. They fail because the framework is not connected to a decision anyone cares about. RiskCraft is our attempt to close that gap — practically, honestly, and without vendor spin.

## What you'll find here

- A structured **[zero-to-hero learning path](/learning-path/)** through IT risk — six levels, from vocabulary to leading a programme.
- **Frameworks** (ISO 27005, NIST RMF, COBIT, COSO, FAIR) explained in plain language with worked examples.
- **Operating models** for governance, appetite, KRIs, and reporting.
- **Career notes** on certifications and moving from analyst to leader.

## Who this is for

- **Analysts and engineers** pulled into risk work.
- **Risk, audit, and GRC professionals** who want a clearer operating model.
- **Managers and architects** who must own risk for their systems.
- **Candidates** studying for CRISC, CISM, or CISSP who want the *why*, not just the answers.
