## How posts are organised

Every post carries two coordinates:

- **Level** (0–5) — how deep it sits on the learning path above.
- **Topic** — the domain it belongs to: Foundations, Risk Assessment, Frameworks & Standards, Treatment & Controls, Governance, Third-Party Risk, Cloud & Cyber Risk, Compliance & Audit, Metrics & Reporting, or Culture & Career.

So you can move along the path in order, or jump to a topic when you have a specific problem to solve.

## Who this is for

- **Analysts and engineers** pulled into risk work.
- **Risk, audit, and GRC professionals** who want a clearer operating model.
- **Managers and architects** who must own risk for their systems.
- **Candidates** studying for CRISC, CISM, or CISSP who want the *why*, not just the answers.

## A note on philosophy

Risk management is not about eliminating risk. It is about making **deliberate, informed, documented decisions** about which risks to take. Everything on this site bends toward that idea.
